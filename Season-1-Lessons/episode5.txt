🚀 AI Learning Journey – Episode 5: How Does a Machine Represent Meaning? 🤖

Today, I completed Episode 5 of Namaste AI, where I learned how machines represent and understand meaning using vectors and embeddings.

Here are some key concepts I learned:

🔹 Vectorization
The process of converting information into a numerical vector (an array of numbers).
Why numbers? Because machines can perform mathematical operations on numerical representations.

🔹 Dimensions
When information is converted into a vector, different characteristics or properties are represented as dimensions.

🔹 Embedding
A learned numerical representation of information that captures useful relationships between different items.

🔹 Semantic Similarity
Embeddings help machines identify how closely related two pieces of information are based on their meaning.

🔹 Token Embedding vs Text Embedding

Token Embedding:
A token ID, which is just an identifier, is mapped to a meaningful numerical vector.

Text Embedding:
An entire sentence or piece of text is represented as a numerical vector that captures its meaning.

The basic flow I understood:

Text → Tokens → Embeddings
