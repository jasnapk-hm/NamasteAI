Today, I went through Episode 4 of the Namaste AI course, where I learned about an important concept in LLMs — TOKENS 🧩


🔹 What are Tokens?
Every prompt we give to an LLM is first converted into tokens.
Tokens are small units of text, which can be words, parts of words, characters, or symbols.

🔹 Tokenizer
A tokenizer is responsible for converting our text into tokens. These tokens are then represented as numbers so that the model can process them.

🔹 Vocabulary
Every LLM has its own tokenizer, and each tokenizer has a vocabulary.
Vocabulary is basically the collection of tokens that the tokenizer knows.

🔹 Context Window
The context window is the maximum amount of information an LLM can handle at a time — including what we send as input and what the model generates as output.
